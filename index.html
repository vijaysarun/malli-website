Our story

Why we are called Malli.

Malli is the word for jasmine in several Indian languages, the flower you see at every celebration back home, weddings, get togethers, women wearing it in their hair. It's tradition, a small sign that something good is happening.

In another southern language, Malli also means “come back.” Both meanings are the point.

We want Malli to feel like that, like a celebration, inviting and familiar. And we want every guest to leave already thinking about the next time they'll come back.

  • Authentic FlavorsA journey through India's rich culinary heritage.
  • Elevated ExperienceRefined spaces for meaningful gatherings.
  • People at the HeartWarm hospitality that makes every visit special.
